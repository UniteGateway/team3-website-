Our growth framework

The TEAM3 marketing funnel.

Every campaign is planned against four stages — with the channels and the measures that matter at each one.

  • Visibility: SEO · Social · Content · PR. Measure: Reach & impressions.
  • Engagement: Social media · Video · Influencers · Email. Measure: Engagement rate & visits.
  • Conversion: Search ads · Landing pages · CRO · WhatsApp. Measure: Leads & cost per lead.
  • Growth: Marketing automation · AI personalisation · CRM. Measure: Revenue & lifetime value.
