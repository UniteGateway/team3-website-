About TEAM3

Imagination is where everything begins.

Founded in 2004, TEAM3 began with a vision to bring creative communication and information technology together under one roof.

Our storyThe meaning of 3Vision & missionValuesFounderJourney
Our story

Two decades of creativity and code.

Over the years, TEAM3 developed capabilities across advertising, branding, software development, web technologies, corporate communication, digital marketing and smart systems.

Today, TEAM3 is being reimagined for a new generation of businesses, where artificial intelligence, human creativity, data and technology work together to create meaningful impact.

We believe technology should simplify complexity, creativity should create value, and innovation should deliver measurable business outcomes.

The meaning behind TEAM3

The name TEAM3 itself represents teamwork.

The number “3” represents our clients, our team members and the management. We believe in maintaining good client relationships and employee relationships — a philosophy from the original TEAM3 corporate presentation that continues to form our foundation.
