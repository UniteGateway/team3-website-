The meaning behind TEAM3

TEAM stands for teamwork. 3 for the people behind it.

The number three represents the three pillars of our organisation: our clients, our team members and our management. This philosophy from the original TEAM3 corporate presentation still forms our foundation.

1

Our Clients

Every engagement starts and ends with the client. We build relationships that last — many for well over a decade.

2

Our Team

Designers, engineers, strategists and AI specialists — a strong technical team with deep domain knowledge.

3

Our Management

Leadership that believes in teamwork, good client relationships and good employee relationships.
