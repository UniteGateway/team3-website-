Privacy Policy

Who we are

This website is operated by TEAM3 Solutions, Hyderabad, India (“TEAM3”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you visit this website or contact us. We aim to comply with applicable data protection law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

We collect only the information you choose to give us through our forms — for example your name, company, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, location, the service you are interested in, your message, and any portfolio or profile links you share. We do not ask for sensitive personal data through this website.

Our web host may automatically record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, for security and reliability purposes.

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries, meeting requests, partner registrations and career profiles.
  • To prepare proposals and deliver services you have requested.
  • To keep records of our communication with you.
  • To protect the website against misuse and fraud.

We do not sell your personal data. We will only send you marketing communication where you have agreed to receive it, and you can withdraw that consent at any time.

Sharing

We may share data with service providers who help us operate the website, forms and communication tools, under obligations of confidentiality. We may also disclose data where required by law.

International visitors

We welcome enquiries from around the world. If you contact us from outside India — including from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom — your details are processed in India for the purpose of responding to you. Where laws such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation apply, we process enquiry data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to business requests, and you may exercise the rights those laws give you by contacting us.

Retention

We keep enquiry and recruitment data only for as long as needed for the purpose it was provided for, or as required by law, and then delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance about how it has been handled. To do so, please contact us through the contact page.

Responsible AI Policy

Our commitment

TEAM3 develops sustainable and ethical solutions. We use artificial intelligence to amplify people, not to replace accountability. Every AI system and agent we deliver is designed around the following principles.

Principles

  • Human oversight — human approval for sensitive actions, and clear owners for every AI system.
  • Least privilege — role-based permissions so agents access only the data and systems their task requires.
  • Traceability — audit trails that record what an agent did, with which data, and why.
  • Data protection — confidentiality and privacy by design; client data is not used to train third-party models without explicit agreement.
  • Transparency — people are told when they are interacting with an AI system, and outcomes are explained where possible.
  • Fairness and safety — testing for bias, errors and misuse before and after deployment.
  • Continuous monitoring — ongoing measurement of quality, cost and performance, with the ability to pause any agent.

Model selection

We select AI technologies based on each client’s business requirements, security needs, budget and operational objectives, and we document those choices.
