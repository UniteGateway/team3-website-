Responsible AI Policy

TEAM3 develops sustainable and ethical solutions. Every AI and agent deployment we deliver is built on the following principles:

  • Human approval for sensitive actions
  • Role-based permissions and least-privilege access
  • Audit trails for agent activity
  • Data protection and confidentiality by design
  • Transparent, explainable outcomes where possible
  • Continuous monitoring of agent performance
